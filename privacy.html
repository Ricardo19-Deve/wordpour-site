Word Pour — Privacy Policy

Effective 22 September 2026. Developer: Ricardo Pinheiro, Portugal. Contact: ricardopacheco.pinheiro@hotmail.com

Summary

Word Pour does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address or contacts. Your progress is stored on your device. The app shows advertising through Google AdMob and offers optional in-app purchases through Apple. It does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

Level progress, stars, settings, your word ledger, daily streak and purchase entitlements are saved locally in the app's private storage. A small anonymous event log (which screens you open, levels started and finished, ads shown) is also kept on the device to help improve the game. It is not uploaded unless a future version says so here.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Word Pour uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Google may collect and process data such as your IP address (approximate location), device identifiers, advertising identifier, ad interactions, crash logs and diagnostics, as described in Google's policy. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Word Pour shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before requesting ads; you can decline personalised ads and still play the whole game. If you purchase Remove Ads, banners and interstitials are no longer requested; rewarded ads remain available only when you choose to watch one.

Purchases (Apple)

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Word Pour receives only the product identifier and a transaction receipt; it never sees your payment details. Restore Purchases re-checks your entitlements with Apple.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, Word Pour submits your best scores to leaderboards and reports achievements using Apple's Game Center service under Apple's privacy policy. Game Center is optional.

Children

Word Pour is rated 4+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

You can delete all locally stored data by deleting the app. For questions or requests regarding data processed by Google, see Google's controls in the consent form (Settings › Privacy options, where required) or Google's privacy tools. For anything else, write to the address above.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The effective date at the top will change accordingly.
