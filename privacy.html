Word Pour — Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026. Developer: Ricardo Pinheiro, Portugal. Contact: ricardopacheco.pinheiro@hotmail.com

Summary

Word Pour does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address or contacts. Your game progress is stored on your device. The app shows advertising through Google AdMob and offers optional in-app purchases through Apple.

On iOS, Word Pour uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before Google Mobile Ads is initialized when tracking authorization has not yet been determined. Tracking for third-party advertising is only permitted when the user grants the relevant iOS permission.

If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the game remains fully functional and advertisements may still be shown without access to Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA).

Data stored on your device

Level progress, stars, settings, your word ledger, daily streak and purchase entitlements are saved locally in the app's private storage.

A small event log, such as screens opened, levels started and completed and ads shown, may also be stored locally to support the operation and improvement of the game.

Advertising — Google AdMob

Word Pour uses Google AdMob to provide banner, interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may collect or process information such as approximate location derived from IP address, device identifiers, advertising data, interactions with advertisements, product interactions, crash information, performance information and other diagnostic information.

This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention and related advertising services in accordance with Google's privacy documentation.

In the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland, Word Pour uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where required to obtain or manage advertising consent.

On iOS, after any required regional consent flow, Word Pour checks Apple's App Tracking Transparency authorization status before initializing Google Mobile Ads. If tracking permission has not previously been requested, iOS presents Apple's system tracking permission dialog.

If you grant permission, the advertising SDK may access the advertising identifier and use data for permitted advertising and measurement purposes. If you deny or restrict tracking permission, Word Pour remains fully functional and Google Mobile Ads can continue operating without access to the IDFA.

You can manage Apple's tracking permission later through iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you purchase Remove Ads, banner and interstitial advertisements are no longer requested. Rewarded advertisements remain available only when you explicitly choose to watch one for an in-game benefit.

Purchases — Apple

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Word Pour receives information necessary to identify and validate the purchased product and entitlement. Word Pour does not receive your payment card or banking information.

The Restore Purchases function asks Apple to verify your existing entitlements.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, Word Pour may submit scores to leaderboards and report achievements through Apple's Game Center service. Game Center is optional and is governed by Apple's privacy practices.

Children

Word Pour is rated 4+. The app does not knowingly request or collect a child's name, email address, contacts or other directly identifying information. Advertising and privacy consent are handled through the applicable Apple and Google privacy mechanisms.

Your choices and rights

You can delete locally stored Word Pour game data by deleting the app.

On iOS, you can review or change tracking permission through Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Word Pour.

Advertising consent and privacy controls provided by Google may also be available where required by applicable law.

For information about data processed by Google, please refer to Google's privacy controls and policies.

For questions about Word Pour or this Privacy Policy, contact ricardopacheco.pinheiro@hotmail.com.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Word Pour, its third-party services or applicable privacy requirements change. The effective date at the top of this policy will be updated whenever material changes are made.
